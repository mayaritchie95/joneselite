02 Who we are

A local family,
not a franchise

Jones Elite Roofing & Exteriors Inc. is a family-run business based in Oromocto, New Brunswick. We handle the exterior of your home — metal roofing, siding, decking, windows, doors, garages and more.

We won't do anything to someone's home that we wouldn't do to our own. Every house we work on gets treated as if it were ours, and we don't sacrifice quality for speed or quantity. Our reputation matters to us, and so does the work we leave behind.

If you're a homeowner who wants a project brought to life with as little stress and inconvenience as possible — you're in the right place. That's the whole point of how we work: Do It Right The First Time.
